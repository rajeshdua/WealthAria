COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reach out

A few quick answers to common questions about WealthAria.

Is WealthAria financial advice?

WealthAria is an educational platform. Our content and calculators are designed to help users understand financial concepts and should not be treated as personalised financial advice.

Can I suggest a new calculator?

Absolutely. Send us the idea through the contact form and tell us what you would like the calculator to help you understand.

Can businesses partner with WealthAria?

Yes. Use the partnership option to tell us about your organisation, proposed collaboration and what you have in mind.

I found a website problem. What should I do?

Select “Website issue” and include the page, device and a short description of what went wrong. A screenshot can also help.
